IT Command Center

I built an IT support dashboard that brings help desk tickets, equipment inventory, endpoint records, and troubleshooting documentation into one workspace.

Demo walkthrough

  1. Triage tickets

    Start on the overview to see urgent, escalated, and deferred work. Open the ticket queue to review what needs attention.

  2. Inspect an endpoint

    Open a sample device to see its platform, assigned configuration, and last check-in.

  3. Check inventory and find a fix

    Look up equipment in inventory and use the knowledge base to find troubleshooting documentation.
